Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by store and site traffic, average ticket and comparable sales, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by cost of goods and supplier terms and distribution and fulfilment cost per unit. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question A largely fixed store and distribution base against thin unit margin, so small comparable-sales moves swing profit. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into few operators of national scale; advantage compounds with purchasing volume and distribution density, where the contest is most direct with social commerce platforms, delivery services and The shopper buying the same basket somewhere cheaper. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is what to charge, and for what, without losing more store and site traffic than the price gains. The lever management holds is sourcing and private label, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
