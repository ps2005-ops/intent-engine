Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by users and their engagement, impressions or ad load per user and price per impression, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by infrastructure and compute per user served and content moderation and trust operations. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Serving one more impression costs almost nothing, so incremental revenue is unusually valuable — and the same fixed base makes an engagement decline expensive quickly. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into a few platforms holding most of the inventory, competing for advertiser budget against every other channel that can prove return, where the contest is most direct with Within Google Services, The advertiser spending the budget on its own channels and Another surface holding the same attention hour.
