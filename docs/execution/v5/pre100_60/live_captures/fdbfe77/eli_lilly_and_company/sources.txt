There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH per approved product: development cost is sunk before the first sale and marginal supply cost is comparatively small. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into protected positions of limited life: approval and exclusivity confer pricing power until they expire, where the contest is most direct with generic competition to Verzenio, generic competition to Mounjaro and large number of multinational pharmaceutical companies. The lever management holds is pipeline and development priorities, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
