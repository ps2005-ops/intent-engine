# Offer one-pager — Free Structural Analysis for Founders — DRAFT

**What you get**: a readable report on one decision or market situation
you're weighing — the structural conditions your situation actually
exhibits, the documented historical mechanisms they match (with named
sources and cited episodes), what would change the read, and — printed
just as prominently — what didn't match and what couldn't be verified.

**What it costs**: nothing. You're early users of a system whose
prediction ledger is deliberately public and young; free analyses are how
it earns attention it can't yet claim on track record.

**What makes it different**
1. *Tested restraint.* The extraction step passed a reliability protocol
   for exactly the failure every analyst has: forcing a confident story
   onto an ambiguous situation. When your case is unclear, the report
   says so — that answer shipped in test and ships in production.
2. *Documented precedent, not vibes.* Mechanisms come from a citation-
   carrying library (Panic of 1907 → 2022 hiking cycle), matched by
   deterministic rules.
3. *Honesty markers as features.* UNAVAILABLE labels, loud DATA GAPS,
   "none matched — and that's the finding."
4. *A ledger instead of a highlight reel.* Forward claims are recorded
   append-only and graded by code against real data. **We claim no
   predictive accuracy today — 0 predictions resolved yet — and the
   calibration record will be public either way, against baselines we
   have to beat honestly.**

**What we ask back**: 15 minutes of feedback on the report, and — only if
you found it useful — permission to quote you.

**How to start**: one paragraph describing the decision. Report back in
[X days].
