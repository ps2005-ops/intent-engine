Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by billable headcount or capacity, utilisation and rate or yield, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by compensation and recruiting and training. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question LOW to MODERATE: cost scales with the people delivering, so margin comes from utilisation and rate, not from volume. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into competition on capability, reputation and coverage; capacity is added by hiring or by adding routes, where the contest is most direct with international postal services, motor carriers and The customer's own staff doing the work. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is what to charge, and for what, without losing more billable headcount or capacity than the price gains. The lever management holds is hiring and capacity plan, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
