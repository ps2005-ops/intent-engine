Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by volume, price and promotional depth and product and channel mix, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by input and commodity cost and freight and logistics. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Input cost is largely variable, brand investment is largely discretionary. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into brand-led competition for shelf and attention against private label and against other branded entrants, where the contest is most direct with Keurig Dr Pepper Inc, Danone S.A and Retailer own-label in the same aisle. Where the strategy is exposed the second buyer arrives with requirements the first never had. The lever management holds against that is pricing and promotion.
