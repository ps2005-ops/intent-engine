Cloudflare, Inc. — the full story Home · Your analyses · Guest demo session Leave demo The full story Cloudflare, Inc. — the whole picture, in order Cloudflare, Inc. is a software platform business that runs on recurring software subscription: revenue is contracted and renews, so the installed base carries next period's revenue before any new sale. Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by customer count, seats or usage per customer and list and realised price, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by sales and marketing to acquire an account and engineering headcount. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Delivery cost rises far more slowly than contracted revenue, so incremental revenue is unusually valuable. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into many differentiated vendors competing on capability and switching cost rather than on price alone, where the contest is most direct with On-premises network hardware vendors, Point solution vendors and network security vendors. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is what to charge, and for what, without losing more customer count than the price gains. The lever management holds is pricing and packaging, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally. On-premises network hardware vendors — this category matches on capability rather than on headline price, because a public price cut is visible to its whole installed base and a feature is not. The counter available here is hold list price and compete on the part of the job they have to build rather than buy. and the thing to watch for is discounting that appears in their public pricing rather than in individual deals. What should happen next move on pricing and packaging at a size that can be reversed inside one planning cycle, and instrument it so the result is readable before the next commitment. run the change on one segment or one cohort, hold the rest constant, and read customer count against the unchanged group for one full cycle — that isolates the effect without committing the whole book. if the direction is not visible by the next review, the assumption under the decision has failed.
