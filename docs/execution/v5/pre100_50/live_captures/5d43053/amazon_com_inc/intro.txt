AMZN · SEC CIK 0001018724 Amazon.com, Inc. is a retail business that runs on several distinct businesses under one owner, where the engine that carries the profit is not the one that carries the revenue — so a consolidated margin describes none of them. Its position is contested directly by a specialist doing one engine better than the bundle, and customers can substitute publishers and distributors.
