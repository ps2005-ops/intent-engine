# Structural Analysis — [Client company]
*Prepared [date] · every claim carries its source · what we can't verify,
we say we can't verify*

## 1. Your situation, as we read it
[2-4 sentences restating the client's decision/market situation in plain
language — confirmed with the client before analysis, so the analysis
answers the right question.]

## 2. Structural conditions actually present
[The extracted trigger-condition profile, each with the sentence(s) of the
client's own description that supports it. Conditions we considered and
did NOT select are as informative as the ones we did.]

> **If the extraction selected little or nothing**: "Your situation, as
> described, does not clearly exhibit the structural conditions in our
> taxonomy. That is a finding, not a failure — it means the documented
> crisis mechanisms we track are not obviously your risk. We'd rather
> tell you that than force a dramatic story."

## 3. Mechanisms possibly in play
[For each matched mechanism: name, confidence tier, matched conditions
(provenance), the causal chain in business language, and the cited
historical instance — e.g. "Supply-shock propagation (well documented) —
matched on your single-supplier dependency; historical instance: the
2020-23 semiconductor shortage."]

> **If none matched**: the "none matched — and that's the finding" card,
> verbatim house style.

## 4. What we could not verify
[UNAVAILABLE items and DATA GAPS, loudly. Empty only when genuinely
nothing was missing.]

## 5. What would change our read
[The 2-3 concrete observations that would flip a condition from absent to
present or vice versa — the falsifiable edge of the analysis.]

## Method note (fixed text, every report)
Structural conditions are extracted against a fixed taxonomy by a system
tested for restraint on ambiguous input; mechanisms are matched by
deterministic rules from a library of documented historical episodes with
named sources. We do not claim predictive accuracy: our forward
predictions live in an append-only ledger graded automatically against
real data, and its calibration record is public as it accumulates.
