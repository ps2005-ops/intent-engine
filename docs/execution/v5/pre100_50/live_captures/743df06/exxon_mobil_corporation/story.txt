Exxon Mobil Corporation — the full story Home · Your analyses · Guest demo session Leave demo The full story Exxon Mobil Corporation — the whole picture, in order Exxon Mobil Corporation is a materials energy business that runs on production of an undifferentiated output sold at a price the producer does not set. Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by produced volume, realised commodity price and grade or quality of output, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by energy and fuel and labour. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Cost per unit is largely fixed against a price that is not, so margin swings by more than price does. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into price-taking producers differentiated by cost position and by the quality and life of the resource base, where the contest is most direct with Substitute materials at the customer's plant, Automation absorbing the task itself and Agnico Eagle Mines Limited. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is where the next increment of capital goes, given what the same capital would earn against produced volume elsewhere. The lever management holds is production plan, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
