Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by spread on assets and liabilities, transaction and fee volume and balance or asset growth, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by cost of funds and credit and fraud losses. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH on volume and LEVERED on the balance sheet: the same movement reaches earnings through both margin and credit. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into regulated, scale-driven, and competitive on price of funds, distribution and trust, where the contest is most direct with Automated Clearing House, Card Member and issuers. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is what to charge, and for what, without losing more spread on assets and liabilities than the price gains. The lever management holds is pricing of assets and liabilities, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
