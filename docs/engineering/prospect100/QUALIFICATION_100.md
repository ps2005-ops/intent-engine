## Research report (D)

- **Independently corroborated (≥2 independent relevant origins): 0.** Single independent origin: 10. Company-only or evidence-limited: 85.
- **Where the decision came from:** CLASS_PRIOR_ONLY 50, NO_DECISION 28, EVIDENCE_LED 9, CLASS_PRIOR_REINFORCED_BY_EVIDENCE 8. CLASS_PRIOR_ONLY means the decision question is the standing question for the business model, labelled as such on the page ("the reading follows from how this kind of business works"), not derived from the company's own evidence.
- **Why independence is low:** the only independent channel is SEC full-text search for filings by other registrants that name the company. About 85 of the 100 are private and rarely named in filings. Review sites are proposed but refuse automated readers. No licensed news/search channel is connected; adding one is a product and licensing decision, not something this pass did.
- **What the repairs changed in research quality:** a SPAC's 10-K no longer counts as Matillion's independent evidence (board-seat biographies are career facts; one sentence repeated is one passage; an independent-class filing is read only for the passage naming the subject). Matillion's live DECISION_GRADE_READING was therefore a false positive; locally it is an honest abstention. B2B software no longer gets a consumer-spending macro mechanism from an industry list. Barracuda's documents are no longer gzip bytes; Altana's and BlackLine's homepages are now readable (body past 2 MB).

Epistemic outcomes on the final local pass (95 analyses): DEFENSIBLE_ABSTENTION 50, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_HANDLED_CORRECTLY 25, DECISION_GRADE_READING 10, RETRIEVAL_LIMITATION_HANDLED_CORRECTLY 9, PRODUCT_DEFECT 1.
