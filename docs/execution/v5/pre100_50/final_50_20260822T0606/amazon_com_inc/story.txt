Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by store and site traffic, average ticket and comparable sales, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by cost of goods and supplier terms and distribution and fulfilment cost per unit. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question A largely fixed store and distribution base against thin unit margin, so small comparable-sales moves swing profit. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here.
