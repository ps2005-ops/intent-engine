WMT · USA · walmart.com · SEC CIK 0000104169 Walmart Inc. is a consumer business that runs on sale of other people's products at deliberately thin margin, where the return comes from scale in buying and from how fast inventory turns rather than from price premium. Its position is contested directly by social commerce platforms and delivery services, and customers can substitute the shopper buying the same basket somewhere cheaper, and the economics that decide the contest are moderate operating leverage — a largely fixed store and distribution base against thin unit margin, so small comparable-sales moves swing profit.
