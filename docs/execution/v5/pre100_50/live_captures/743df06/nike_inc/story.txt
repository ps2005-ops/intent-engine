Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by volume, price and promotional depth and product and channel mix, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by input and commodity cost and freight and logistics. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Input cost is largely variable, brand investment is largely discretionary. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into brand-led competition for shelf and attention against private label and against other branded entrants, where the contest is most direct with Li Ning, Under Armour and Retailer own-label in the same aisle. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is what to charge, and for what, without losing more volume than the price gains. The lever management holds is pricing and promotion, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
