Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by unit volume, product and node mix and average selling price, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by manufacturing and foundry cost and yield. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH and two-sided: fixed manufacturing cost rewards utilisation and punishes an idle line. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into few credible suppliers; position turns on process capability and on access to manufacturing capacity, where the contest is most direct with Keeping the existing fleet running longer, Rental and used equipment in place of a new purchase and Automation absorbing the task itself. Where the strategy is exposed fixed cost is committed in large increments against demand that arrives in small ones. The lever management holds against that is capacity commitment.
