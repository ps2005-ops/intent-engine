Where this sits against the alternatives

The customer's own engineering: Not a company — the customer building it themselves, read from how this business model works rather than from a retrieved source. Turns the decision into buy-versus-build, where the customer's own engineering time is the competing price and it is not on anyone's budget line. The customer's own engineering gets funded for one quarter, ships something that works, and then meets the maintenance cost nobody budgeted. Response likelihood: not a response — it is a decision made inside the customer, on the customer's calendar. Price against the fully-loaded cost of the build including the second year, and sell to the person who owns that budget. Renewing nothing and keeping the current process: Not a company — the customer doing nothing, read from how this business model works rather than from a retrieved source.
