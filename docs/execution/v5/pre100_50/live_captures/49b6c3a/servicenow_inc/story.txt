Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by customer count, seats or usage per customer and list and realised price, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by sales and marketing to acquire an account and engineering headcount. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question Delivery cost rises far more slowly than contracted revenue, so incremental revenue is unusually valuable. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into many differentiated vendors competing on capability and switching cost rather than on price alone, where the contest is most direct with The buyer's own engineering team, broad range of solutions and enterprise application software vendors. Where the strategy is exposed businesses that subsidise each other are managed together and disclosed apart. The lever management holds against that is pricing and packaging.
