Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by unit volume, product and node mix and average selling price, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by manufacturing and foundry cost and yield. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH and two-sided: fixed manufacturing cost rewards utilisation and punishes an idle line. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into few credible suppliers; position turns on process capability and on access to manufacturing capacity, where the contest is most direct with Huawei Technologies Co, Open-source AI and Keeping the existing fleet running longer. Where the strategy is exposed fixed cost is committed in large increments against demand that arrives in small ones. The lever management holds against that is capacity commitment. How it got to here The public record this analysis holds for NVIDIA Corporation runs from 2021-04-23 to 2026-08-17, across 90 company filings, including 5 full annual accounts — the documents in which a company restates what it is. What the argument is actually about fixed cost is committed in large increments against demand that arrives in small ones. Utilisation, order books and take-or-pay terms are not public. it is the parameter that decides whether a capacity commitment is worth making now or worth deferring, and the two answers lead to different plans. What the other side does NVIDIA Corporation names it as a competitor in its own filing — our current competitors include: suppliers and licensors of hardware and software for discrete and integrated GPUs, custom chips and other accelerated computing solutions, includin. Huawei Technologies Co matches on capability rather than on headline price, because a public price cut is visible to its whole installed base and a feature is not. The counter available here is hold list price and compete on the part of the job they have to build rather than buy. and the thing to watch for is discounting that appears in their public pricing rather than in individual deals.
