## Where the loop is currently open

Session 3 closed **epistemic attribution**: the engine can now say which
evidence changed which knowledge object (`KnowledgeEffect`, written at real
state-change seams).

The loop is open at **policy attribution**. The engine cannot yet answer:

    why did we choose to seek that evidence?
    what else could we have searched?
    did the choice pay off?
    should we choose differently next time?

The research log is reconstructed from evidence that *survived*, so every
action that returned nothing is structurally absent and every rate computed
from it is biased toward success. Until choices are logged BEFORE the call,
research-policy learning is learning from a success log.

That is why PROGRAM A (research observability) is the root of the graph: it is
not the most interesting work, it is the work the largest number of other nodes
depend on.
