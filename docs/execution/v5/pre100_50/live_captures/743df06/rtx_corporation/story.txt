Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by orders and backlog, delivery or production rate and aftermarket and services on the installed base, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by supply chain and component availability and labour and skills. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH against production rate: fixed cost is absorbed by rate, so a rate change moves margin more than revenue. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into duopoly or oligopoly with certification and installed-base barriers that make entry slow and displacement rare, where the contest is most direct with Independent service and will-fit parts, Deferring replacement and rebuilding instead and Automation absorbing the task itself. Where the strategy is exposed the second buyer arrives with requirements the first never had. The lever management holds against that is production rate.
