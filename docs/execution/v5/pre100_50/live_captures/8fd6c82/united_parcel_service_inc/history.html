What was true thenObserved

What management didObserved

The choice is legible in the figures it filed: revenue growth slowed and the margin narrowed with it — cost was not coming out as fast as demand. For this kind of business the levers available were hiring and capacity plan, utilisation management and pricing and rate card.

What the market expectedModelled

What happenedObserved

Better alternativeCounterfactual

Stop paying for growth that has already stopped arriving, and take the cost out before the market prices the slowdown in. Full participation in the recovery at existing cost.

Why it might have workedCounterfactual

Capacity is people and routes and both take a year to rebuild, so cutting into a dip trades a small saving now for the inability to serve the recovery.

What could have invalidated itCounterfactual

Cutting into a cyclical trough removes the capacity needed to serve the recovery, and capacity of this kind takes a year to rebuild. The assumption it rests on is that the slowdown is structural rather than a single weak period — the distinction the whole decision turns on, and one the next two quarters settle — and that was checkable at the time, which is what makes this an argument rather than a preference.

Better alternativeCounterfactual

Press the advantage: fund the next expansion while the cost base is still absorbing the current one. Full participation in the recovery at existing cost.

Why it might have workedCounterfactual

Capacity is people and routes and both take a year to rebuild, so cutting into a dip trades a small saving now for the inability to serve the recovery.

What could have invalidated itCounterfactual

An expansion funded on the strength of one good year is a bet that the year was structural, and a cycle turning underneath it is exactly what makes it look structural. The assumption it rests on is that the cost base absorbing this year's growth will absorb next year's, which holds while the margin is widening and stops the moment it is not — and that was checkable at the time, which is what makes this an argument rather than a preference.
