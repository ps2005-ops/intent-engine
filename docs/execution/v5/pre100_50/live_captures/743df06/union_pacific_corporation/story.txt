Where the money actually comes from Revenue is decided by contracted or regulated volume, tariff or rate and asset base in service, and whether that revenue becomes margin is decided by financing cost and depreciation. There is no other lever on the top line. Why growth and profitability are one question HIGH and financed: the cost base is capital and interest, so the cost of money is an operating variable. So a decision that moves volume moves margin with it, and the two cannot be planned separately here. What the market rewards It sells into few operators, high barriers, and returns set as much by contract and regulation as by competition, where the contest is most direct with transportation providers, motor carriers and The counterparty self-supplying behind the meter. Where the strategy is exposed The open question is where the next increment of capital goes, given what the same capital would earn against contracted or regulated volume elsewhere. The lever management holds is capital programme and sequencing, and the cost of pulling it is that the effect is visible to competitors before it is measurable internally.
