# Founder cold-outreach variants — DRAFTS ONLY (workstream 5)

*Nothing sends without per-message founder approval. The
job-application-agent's dry-run-vs-real-send wall applies identically:
drafts may be prepared end-to-end, transmission is a human act, every
send/reply/outcome lands in the append-only tracking ledger (schema in
tracking_ledger_schema.md). Claim rule: same trace table as
landing_page_copy.md; no accuracy claims.*

## Variant 1 — short cold DM (LinkedIn/X, ≤450 chars)

> Hi [name] — I run a structural-analysis system that reads a founder's
> market situation against documented historical mechanisms (supply
> shocks, price wars, credit contagion...), and — unusual for this space —
> it's tested to say "nothing clearly matches" when that's the truth.
> I'm offering free analyses to [segment] founders right now. One
> paragraph about your situation in → readable report with sources back.
> Interested?

## Variant 2 — cold email

**Subject options (pick per recipient):** "structural read on
[company]'s market — free, sources included" · "the analysis that's
allowed to say 'no clear match'"

> Hi [name],
>
> I've built an analysis system for founder decisions — market entry,
> pricing response, expansion timing — that works differently from most:
>
> - It extracts which structural conditions your situation *actually*
>   exhibits, against a fixed taxonomy, and it's been reliability-tested
>   to select nothing when your case is genuinely ambiguous — no forced
>   narratives.
> - Matches are made against a library of documented historical
>   mechanisms, every entry cited (1907 through 2022), by deterministic
>   rules.
> - What can't be verified is labeled UNAVAILABLE. Data gaps are flagged,
>   not smoothed over. Transparent methodology is the product.
>
> I'm doing free structural analyses for a small number of founders while
> the system's public prediction ledger accumulates its track record —
> which is to say plainly: I'm not claiming predictive accuracy, and the
> honest version of that sentence is the reason to try it.
>
> If you send me a paragraph on a decision you're weighing, you'll get a
> readable report back within [X days]. Worst case, you learn what a
> restraint-tested analysis declines to claim about your market.
>
> — Pratham

## Variant 3 — follow-up (one only, ~5 days later)

> Hi [name] — closing the loop on the free structural analysis offer. If
> the timing's wrong, no further nudges from me. If useful context helps:
> here's a sample report [link to lead-magnet template output] — the
> "what we could not verify" section is the part most founders say they
> haven't seen an analyst put in writing before.

## Rules applied to all variants

One follow-up maximum. No fabricated personalization — [segment]/[name]
fields are filled from real research or left generic. No urgency
theater, no invented social proof, no performance claims. Every claim
traces to the shared table (T:1–T:6).
