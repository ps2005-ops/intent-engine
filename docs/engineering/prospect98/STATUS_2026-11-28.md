## Structural limits under the ORIGINAL rubric (not lowered, not worked around)

* **History**: level A requires a filed multi-year series. 85 of 98 companies
  are private or former filers; their best original grade is BOUNDED. A
  bounded former-filer history (Alteryx, Coupa, OneStream, Domo) is designed,
  not built: Domo's former CIK now files as a shell, so a bare CIK would
  misattribute.
* **Economics**: PASS needs the decision to be evidence-led. Widening the
  evidence phrase table with pricing-page furniture would manufacture that
  for every company with a pricing page; refused (see register).
* **Independence**: 27 development companies have no independent origin and
  every one reads DISCOVERY_EXHAUSTED on the 16 verified publisher feeds. That
  is a channel-coverage ceiling for specialist private companies; GDELT is
  the next channel to test in-run (wire-copy rejection must be proven first).
