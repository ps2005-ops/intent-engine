01

The defect

Two intelligence products had diverged for 202 and 371 commits from a common ancestor. One asks what is happening around a company; the other asks which economic mechanisms are changing. They shared a repository and shared nothing else.

market.macro_state already held a dated, revision-aware, three-times-per-fact reading of the economy. market.transmission already conditioned it on a specific company. market.company_exposure already refused to infer an exposure from a sector. All of it was correct — and all of it sat inside intent_engine.market, which founder code may not import and must not, because “trading internals cannot reach a founder’s screen” is a property of the import graph rather than a promise about what people remember.

So the better economic picture was structurally unreachable from the surface that needed it, and every company analysis re-derived a worse one from whatever documents that company happened to publish.

A module nothing calls is inert, and this repository has shipped inert repairs before. Two steps were added to both the day and night market cycles, the founder analysis path reads and writes the core in the same pass, and /learning answers eleven questions from it.

Two branches inside those patterns were dead

\b(\d+\s*%|percent|majority)\b could never match a percentage: the alternative ends on %, a non-word character, and the trailing boundary then demands a word character — " of revenue" is not one. Only the spelled-out forms ever rated, and the numeric form is what filings use. Separately, capital expenditure never matched the plural every filing writes.

Both fixed in both copies, with a test that fails if the copies ever drift apart.

Found by reading the first published state

Direction was the sign of a level

All five measured conditions read UP — including a consumer price index of 333.918, a number greater than zero in every month that has ever existed. Uniformity across every condition is the instrument tell: a real economy does not move one way in five out of five. The change was computable the whole time, from 23–46 dated observations per condition already sitting in the graph.

Found by auditing importers

acceleration had no producer

The module was complete, tested, and imported by nothing. Rolling windows over a history nobody writes are windows over an empty list: every one would have reported INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY forever, and nobody could have told that from a genuinely young engine. Its own suite passed against fixtures the whole time.

The publish step now records real counts each cycle — evidence_new is content this core had not seen before rather than rows appended, and belief_movement is the size of the probability changes rather than the count of them, so a velocity measure cannot be gamed by running more often.

Cycle 3, verbatim: INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY · 1 cycle(s) of history — 3 new of 554 evidence nodes (novelty 0.0054). Both halves matter: the counter refused to call 551 re-reads new, and the window refused to call one cycle a trend.

Found by cycle 1, in the report itself

Two steps ran green and invisible

econ_publish and econ_aggregate completed successfully for a full cycle and appeared in neither the markdown nor the JSON, because the cycle report reads named keys and nobody had added them. A step whose result is not in the record has no history, and “it ran” is the only thing anyone can ever say about it.

05

What is deliberately refused

  • Demo search queries as a trading signal.No function in the bridge takes a query, a session, a visitor, or a count of any of them — asserted structurally, by parameter name. It would be a signal about who linked to the product this week, and a manipulation surface for anyone who can type into a public box.
  • Live capital.LiveBrokerAdapter.__init__ raises. Enabling it needs an authorisation object that does not exist anywhere in this repository, so the change is a visible addition rather than a flag flip.
  • Causal language below evidence level 3.A different sentence is constructed. There is no argument, flag or override that produces “causes” from a level-2 edge — including through a chain, where the weakest link decides.
  • Any accuracy figure before 30 resolved forward predictions.Status reads PRE_CALIBRATION and the headline names the shortfall. Prose that implies performance is refused by the same check.
  • Tenant-private evidence in a public aggregate.A refusal, never a filter — an aggregate quietly built from private material and reported as smaller is a breach that also lies about its own sample.
  • An exposure inferred from a sector.It produces the same claim for every company in the sector, so the most specific-sounding output carries the least information. The function raises in both copies.
06

What is honestly incomplete

The market engine’s own cycle path still reads headlines through its ledger-shaped exposure reader; the shared capability is used by the founder path, not yet by that one. Twelve causal families carry no recorded mechanism, so 100 of 151 beliefs cannot cross. No forward expectation has been written to the shared core, so calibration sits at n=0 and will stay there until the belief→expectation producer writes into econ rather than only into the market ledger. Six series are keyed and six are unavailable — OIS, cross-currency basis, the VIX complex, dealer gamma — each carrying its reason, none synthesised.

Dealer gamma is the one worth naming twice: its sign is unknown to this deployment, and an unknown sign leaves the short-gamma loop not armed rather than assumed. It is the loop a guess would matter most in.
