# Session Prompt — V2.0: Founder Growth Studio

*Recorded 2026-07-23 from human review. Status: NEEDS HUMAN START, and only
after V1.0.1 freezes V1. This is where the project changes from building
the product to building the company around the product.*

## Mission

**Reuse the existing Marketing and Growth systems to autonomously plan and
continuously improve the marketing of Founder Intelligence itself.**

The first customer of the Growth Studio is our own company. Only after it
proves itself internally can it eventually become a customer-facing
capability.

This is not "build a marketing system." V2.0 **reuses**: the Marketing
subsystem, the Growth subsystem, Research, Analytics, CRM, Personal AI, and
AgentOS. The only truly new work is **orchestrating them around one
client: Founder Intelligence.**

## Core responsibilities (planning, never execution)

SEO planning; website optimization; Product Hunt planning; Indie Hackers
planning; LinkedIn strategy; X strategy; Reddit strategy; newsletter
planning; content planning; campaign planning; audience research;
competitor monitoring; growth analytics; experiment management; product
messaging; launch planning; funnel analysis.

## It does NOT

- publish;
- send emails;
- post to social media;
- modify the website;
- launch campaigns.

Those belong to **V2.5 — Execution Layer**.

## The Creative Strategy Loop (centerpiece)

```text
Observe
    ↓
Research
    ↓
Understand audience
    ↓
Generate growth hypotheses
    ↓
Create messaging strategies
    ↓
Create creative concepts
    ↓
Generate channel-specific drafts
    ↓
Human approval
    ↓
Measure
    ↓
Learn
    ↓
Update growth memory
    ↓
Repeat
```

It is a **learning loop, not a content generator**.

## Product Intelligence Loop

The Growth Studio continuously answers questions like: What messaging
resonates? Which landing page sections are ignored? Which founder questions
repeat? Which objections appear most often? Which evidence convinces users?
Which section of the report is expanded most? Which insights are
remembered? Which features are never used?

That information feeds Product, Research, and Executive — always through
existing boundaries.

## Growth Memory

Append-only memory of: hypotheses; experiments; campaigns; messaging
iterations; audience insights; content performance; funnel changes; lessons
learned. **No silent overwrites.**

## Completion gate

The Growth Studio can wake up each morning and produce something like:

> Yesterday's Product Hunt page converted better than expected. LinkedIn
> posts about executive blind spots outperformed feature posts. Founder
> interviews repeatedly asked about competitor analysis. Three messaging
> experiments are ready for review. Based on the last seven days, the
> homepage hero is likely the next bottleneck.

Everything remains **proposal-first and approval-gated**.

## Roadmap after V2.0

- V1.0.1 — Launch Readiness & Product Polish (precedes V2.0)
- V2.0 — Founder Growth Studio (planning, learning, Creative Strategy Loop)
- V2.5 — Execution Layer (approved publishing and actions)
- V3.0 — Continuous Company Operator (long-term autonomous operating model)

Nothing fundamental in T019–T023.5 changes. The architecture has earned
stability; future work builds on top of it, not rewrites it.
