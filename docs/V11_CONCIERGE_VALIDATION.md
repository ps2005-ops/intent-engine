# V1.1 — Concierge validation package and workflow (non-automated)

The report itself is the first credible outreach artifact. Nothing here
is automated; nothing sends; no contact information is scraped.

## Private export contents (assembled from an accepted report)

Company name · analysis date · approved evidence scope (the exact
source list) · one strong supported observation · one evidence-backed
company-vs-market difference · one possible question for the founder ·
the most important limitation · private report link or export.

Example (from the Plausible acceptance run — synthetic example of the
format, not sent to anyone):

> Based on the public pages analyzed, your homepage emphasizes
> lightweight, cookie-free analytics, while the approved external
> evidence emphasizes reliability and simplicity of day-to-day use.
> That difference may be intentional, but it appears consistently
> across the approved sources.

No aggressive sales language. This is an analysis artifact, not a
marketing email.

## Reusable outreach template (manual use only)

> Hi [Name] — I built an outside-in analysis of [Company] using only
> the public pages listed in the report. One pattern stood out:
> [supported observation]. The evidence is linked and the report is
> explicit about what it cannot determine. I thought it might be useful
> to you. Would you be open to seeing the private report?

No unsupported personalization. Never claim the founder has a problem.
Never send automatically.

## Post-build validation sequence (§44, human-executed)

1. Analyze 10–20 public companies manually (live network).
2. Verify all major claims yourself.
3. Select the five strongest reports.
4. Identify reachable founders or operators.
5. Contact them with one real observation about their company.
6. Offer the full private report.
7. Ask for feedback only after they show interest.
8. Record corrections and requested evidence sources.
9. Improve the product based on repeated problems.
10. Feed real product behavior into V2.0 only after enough real usage
    exists — and classify traffic (developer / internal_test /
    manual_acceptance / early_user) so the Growth Studio never reads
    internal test clicks as customer validation.

## V2.5 boundary

V2.5 remains NEEDS HUMAN START. It may begin only after real-domain
analysis works reliably in live use, several real companies have been
analyzed, founders have received actual reports, failure/correction
patterns are understood, and V2.0 has processed real, classified,
non-test observations with at least one manually reviewed proposal.
